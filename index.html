Weaving Quality with Care

Based in Karur, the home textile hub of South India, Aasee Export specializes in crafting premium cotton and linen fabrics. We thoughtfully convert these fabrics into high-quality cushion covers, chair-pads, table linen, kitchen linen and bags.

Operating with our own integrated dyeing and weaving units, we closely monitor every stage of production to ensure exceptional consistency, robust quality control, and reliable delivery timelines.
