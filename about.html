Philosophy

Our Purpose

Direction

Vision & Mission

Charting our course for meaningful and sustainable global partnerships.

Our Vision

To leverage our decades of manufacturing expertise to develop distinctive home-textile creations and expand our global presence through innovation, quality, and scale.

Our Mission

Our mission is to be a trusted home-textile manufacturing partner by delivering consistent quality, maintaining reliable production timelines, and providing responsive service to meet the evolving expectations of customers worldwide.

Values

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our relationships with employees, clients, and the environment.

Empowering the Working Community

We believe our people are the foundation of our success. We are committed to providing a safe, respectful, and supportive work environment where our workforce is valued, cared for, and empowered to contribute their best.

Adaptability & Innovation

We embrace creativity and remain open to exploring new designs and product ideas. By encouraging fresh perspectives and collaborative development, we work closely with our customers to co-create innovative home-textile creations.

Reliability & Transparency

We build long-term relationships through honesty, transparency, and open communication. By understanding our customers’ requirements, we strive to consistently meet expectations in quality standards and production timelines.

Sustainability

We are committed to responsible manufacturing practices by working primarily with natural fibers such as cotton and linen and maintaining a clean supply chain. We also incorporate recycled materials, including recycled cotton and polyester, to support more sustainable production.

Established in 1995, Aasee Export is a leading manufacturer and exporter of premium Cotton and Linen home textiles based in Karur, India.
